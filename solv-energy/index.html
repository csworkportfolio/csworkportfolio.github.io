How I Modernized SOLV Energy's Web Presence and Built Power Apps That Streamlined Internal Workflows

Internal Tools & External Web for SOLV Energy

SOLV Energy is a lifecycle partner for utility-scale solar, storage, and energy infrastructure across the United States. With more than 20 GW of solar and storage under management and a fast-growing public profile (Nasdaq: MWH), SOLV Energy needed a digital presence that could keep pace with a company building, operating, and announcing at industry scale. This case study covers how I supported and built that digital presence across web, internal tools, graphic design, and video.

THE PROBLEM

A renewable energy company has a harder communications job than most. SOLV Energy's audiences include investors, customers, prospective hires, craft workers, community partners, and thousands of employees in offices and on jobsites across the country. Each audience needs different proof points, different formats, and different channels. Communications systems had not kept pace with that growth. The SOLV Energy website needed steady, accurate updates as new projects, leadership news, and milestones landed. Internal teams needed faster, lighter ways to share approved content to their own networks. Visual storytelling needed to keep up with a project pipeline running nationally and in dozens of states at once.

THE SOLUTION

I built a digital backbone that handled the day-to-day weight: a continuously updated external website, internal tools that turned approved content into employee-shareable assets, graphic design at the cadence project announcements demanded, and video work that brought the work on the ground into focus. Each piece was designed to scale with the company rather than break under it.

Owning the Web Presence and Internal Tooling of a Public Energy Infrastructure Company

A public company moves fast, and its website has to move with it. I maintained and continuously updated solvenergy.com through WordPress, publishing project announcements, leadership additions, earnings news, and partnership wins as they landed. Updates often went live within hours of internal approval, with formatting, imagery, and SEO considerations dialed in before publish.

Beyond the external site, I built and launched an Employee Advocacy platform powered by Power Apps. Built to give SOLV Energy teammates one place to find pre-approved social content to share with their own networks, the platform turned a manual, email-based workflow into a self-serve experience. Employees can browse, preview, and share posts directly to their LinkedIn in seconds.

Together, those efforts kept the company's external presence current while activating the internal community as a force multiplier for visibility, advocacy, and recruiting.

Designing at the Speed of a Public Company's News Cycle

When SOLV Energy goes public, breaks ground, or sends an executive to a conference, the visuals supporting that moment have to be ready as soon as possible and sometimes on the same day. I produced ongoing graphic design across speaker session cards, kudos and recognition posts, project announcements, and case study teasers, working within and gradually extending SOLV Energy's visual system.

Design prioritized clean composition, brand-consistent color palettes anchored on SOLV Energy's signature colors, and a headline hierarchy that made each graphic legible at thumbnail scale on social feeds. Every asset shipped quickly without sacrificing the polish an investor-grade or customer-facing audience expects.

Bringing Field Stories and Project Milestones to Life Through Video

Video is how SOLV Energy makes its work tangible to audiences who will never visit a jobsite. I helped concept, script, and produce multiple social media videos and internal content for project milestones, customer testimonials, and culture moments, with a focus on letting the people doing the work speak for themselves.

Editing stayed minimal and the people stayed central, with on-site capture, light branding overlay, and pacing tuned for both LinkedIn and longer-form internal use.
