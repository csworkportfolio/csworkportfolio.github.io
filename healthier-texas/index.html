How I Designed Campaigns That Drove 40K+ App Installs and Reached 10K Event Attendees

Campaign Design for Public Health & Wellness

Healthier Texas (formerly It’s Time Texas) is a statewide public health organization focused on wellness education, digital outreach, and community engagement. My role spanned social campaigns, video content, and visual storytelling across their web and email platforms during a critical phase of digital expansion.

THE PROBLEM

Healthier Texas runs statewide wellness campaigns that rely on strong visual communication — but in 2020, everything changed. The pandemic halted in-person events, increased urgency for digital outreach, and created a need to show up with clarity, compassion, and flexibility. The creative team needed design that could adapt quickly across platforms while keeping people—not programs—at the center.

THE SOLUTION

I partnered with Healthier Texas to bring their campaigns to life across digital and physical spaces. From virtual summit branding to campaign videos and wellness app promotions, I created design systems, social content, and storytelling assets that helped inform and engage. Every piece — whether a social graphic or web visual—was made to feel real, human, and actionable, even in uncertain times.

Engaging Texans in Health and Wellness Amidst a Pandemic

When the COVID-19 shutdown hit, Healthier Texas (formerly It’s Time Texas) had to reimagine how they showed up for the public — and fast. Within weeks, we launched two digital-first campaigns built to uplift.

I worked to bring these programs to life visually. For Texans Thrive, I helped shape and visualize the website, turning stories of community resilience into a digital experience grounded in warmth and clarity. For Texas Sweats for Pets, I created a full suite of campaign visuals that included social graphics to email headers to paid ad creative — all designed to motivate movement and drive donations.

“We needed something that felt joyful and human, even at a time when things felt heavy.”

Together, these campaigns set the tone for how Healthier Texas would engage through the pandemic — a partner.

Bringing a Statewide Wellness Conference to Life Through Web and Social

The Healthier Texas Summit was one of the state’s largest public health events, and in 2020, it had to go fully virtual. That shift brought new creative challenges: how do you design a digital space that still feels connected, energizing, and real?

I led visual design across the Summit experience, from branded banners and speaker promos to a scalable social template system that could flex with every announcement. I leaned into vibrant color and people-first imagery to create a sense of energy, even through a screen. I didn't want to lose the human element just because it wasn’t being held in person this year.

The result was a visual system that carried through pre-event promotion, livestream assets, and post-event recaps — building consistency and excitement across platforms.

Branding Work for Healthy Texas Week + General Visual Support

Before the pandemic shifted timelines, I had developed the logo and early visuals for Healthy Texas Week, a campaign encouraging healthy eating and movement. While the event was eventually cancelled, the identity laid the groundwork for future brand efforts.

I also created evergreen visual assets for Healthier Texas’s ongoing digital presence: banners, content templates, and social graphics that helped the team stay consistent and responsive.
