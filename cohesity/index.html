How I Led Employee Advocacy for 3,000 Advocates and Cross-Channel Design at Cohesity

Employee Advocacy, AI Localization and Creative for an AI-Powered Data Security Company

Cohesity is an AI-powered data security company trusted by 70% of the Global 500 in more than 140 countries. I led its Champions employee advocacy program, built an AI agent that localized posts for 10+ regions, and designed creative for PR, blogs, newsletters and global events.

Great Product News That Nobody Shared

Enterprise cybersecurity content tends to be dense and written for boardrooms more than for the people who share it. Cohesity had strong product news and real credibility, but its social voice blended in with the rest of enterprise software, and employees weren't sharing posts they didn't connect with. I rebuilt the Champions employee advocacy program from the ground up, starting with a new voice.

Great Product News That Everyone Shared

Every post ended up in the advocates' own feeds, in their own language. I wrote new ones daily and found more through social listening, then loaded each into the Champions Hub, ready to share in one click.

To localize them, I built an AI agent with Claude Code, loaded with Cohesity's brand guidelines and product details and connected directly to the Champions Hub. It adapted the tone for each region as well as the language, across 10+ regions, and its versions came out about 99% ready. I reviewed every one with my team and select Champion advocates, and each fix went back to the agent so it learned.

Leaderboards and the internal competitions I ran kept advocates sharing. The best posts went into the Cohesity Weekly newsletter. I also wrote and edited posts for Cohesity's C-suite and executive team.
