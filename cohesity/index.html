How I Led Employee Advocacy for 3,000 Advocates and Cross-Channel Design at Cohesity

Employee Advocacy and Integrated Creative for an AI-Powered Data Security Leader

Cohesity is a leader in AI-powered data security and management, trusted by 70% of the Global 500 across 140+ countries. Its brand and content needed to feel as human as the people behind it. This case study covers how I helped run Cohesity's Champions employee advocacy program and designed creative across PR, blogs, newsletters and global events.

THE PROBLEM

Enterprise cybersecurity content tends to be dense, technical and written for boardrooms more than for the people who share it, read it and deal with the threats it covers. Cohesity had strong product news, deep customer stories and real industry credibility, but its social voice and visuals blended in with the rest of enterprise software. Employees weren't sharing content they didn't connect with, and the brand was missing chances to reach both its own advocates and outside audiences.

THE SOLUTION

I wrote a new brand voice for the Champions employee advocacy program, closer to Duolingo and Figma on social than to corporate LinkedIn, and ran a daily content engine that gave 3,000 advocates posts they wanted to share. Alongside the advocacy work, I led original design for PR campaigns, blog hero graphics, newsletters and global event activations.

Reframing Cohesity's Voice for an Employee Advocacy Program That Actually Got Shared

I rebuilt the Employee Advocacy program from the ground up and ran its daily content for 3,000 advocate seats worldwide, across a company of 7,000 employees. Beyond writing the core posts, I led the program's redesign, ran internal competitions, expanded it into international markets and connected advocacy work across several departments. I also headed an executive advocacy program, writing and editing posts myself for the Chief Product Officer and the Chief Financial Officer. Employee posts became one of the company's strongest social channels.

Pushing Brand Voice and Visuals Through Creative Holiday Content

Beyond employee advocacy, I supported the creative team on Cohesity's most visible moments: partnership campaigns with Google Cloud, AWS, NVIDIA, Hewlett-Packard and Microsoft, blog hero graphics for executive thought leadership, creative for the weekly Security Spotlight and Empire Strikes Backups newsletters, and event activations. The visuals had to look as modern as consumer design and still carry credibility in enterprise cybersecurity.
