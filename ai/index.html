AI in My Workflow

Claude Code, ChatGPT, Codex, Gemini, Copilot

I'm a digital marketer, and AI is part of how I work every day. It takes on the slow, repetitive tasks and sits as another springboard for ideas.

The workflows below show the moving parts of content machines I've owned and delivered across my roles at QAD Redzone, Cohesity, and Nike, and as a freelancer.

  1. 1PlanEditorial calendar in Monday.com, Jira, and Asana
  2. 2DraftClaude and ChatGPT draft from a knowledge base of rules and ideas
  3. 3RepurposeOne single podcast can become many opportunities for content across mediums
  4. 4LocalizeAdapt for regions, languages, and people around the world
  5. 5OptimizeWrite to develop stronger engagement, traffic, and rankings

What works goes back into the next plan

  • 7xLinkedIn engagement at QAD Redzone, 2% to 14%
  • 3xReels and YouTube reach at QAD Redzone, 20K to 60K
  • 33%More website traffic at QAD Redzone year over year, 56K to 75K sessions
  • 70%Engagement across 3,000 Cohesity Champions
1Plan

From Calendar to First Draft

At QAD Redzone and Cohesity I ran the editorial calendar in Monday.com. AI helped with briefs, first drafts and A/B tests, working from a knowledge base of brand guidelines, AI do's and don'ts, visual examples, product details, customer stories and more. I edited everything before it went out.

Each calendar item had a Generate button I built: it sent the item to Claude through Zapier, with instructions from that knowledge base, and wrote drafts back into the project board.

2Draft

93 Seconds of Takes, Cut to 43

I was given raw footage for a Mars Men project: six takes, two hooks, three overall takes, and a call to action. I started by feeding the transcripts of every take to ChatGPT. The tool timestamped suggested cuts and helped me pick the best hook. I edited the video in Adobe Premiere Pro, used ChatGPT to find B-roll on Pexels that fit what I had in mind, and let AI make the captions for sound-off viewing.

3Repurpose

One Episode, Five Formats

A demonstration on a public episode of Cohesity's Resilience Universe podcast: CEO Sanjay Poonen with Kevin Mandia, 26 minutes. Claude reads the transcript and marks the moments that make sense on their own. I cut each one to the shape and length of the place it will run.

4Localize

One Post, Ready for 10+ Regions

At Cohesity I built an AI agent with Claude Code, loaded it with our style guide and brand guidelines, and built a localization feature into the Champions Hub. Employees could localize one post as they shared it to LinkedIn, or switch on the whole feed and browse every post in their own language and region, graphics included.

The agent adapted the region as much as the language: less US-centric, with the tone set for each place. Its versions came out about 99% ready. I reviewed them with my team and select Champion Advocates, and every fix went back to the agent so it learned.

See the 3D version on the Cohesity page

5Optimize

Written for Search Engines and AI Answers

People type keywords into Google and sometimes ask their AI full questions. GEO and AEO, generative and answer engine optimization, mean writing pages that work for both.

In 2024, I organized QAD Redzone's FAQs and blogs so search engines and AI answers could find them. At Nike I wrote alt text and product tags for both kinds of search: for someone who knows exactly what they want, and for someone who only knows roughly.

Tools I Built With Claude Code and ChatGPT Codex

In my personal time, I build my own productivity apps. I tell each AI tool what I want to create, then test each build hundreds of times and decide how it should look and behave. I use all of these every day.
