Localization, editorial planning, video, repurposing, GEO and AEO, and the tools I build with AI

One Post, Ready for 10+ Regions

At Cohesity I built an AI agent with Claude Code, loaded it with our style guide and brand guidelines, and built a localization feature into the Champions Hub. Employees could localize one post as they shared it to LinkedIn, or switch on the whole feed and browse every post in their own language and region, graphics included.

The agent adapted the region as much as the language: less US-centric, with the tone set for each place. Its versions came out about 99% ready. I reviewed them with my team and select Champion Advocates, and every fix went back to the agent so it learned.

From Calendar to Do's and Don'ts

At QAD Redzone and Cohesity I used AI across the editorial workflow: the content calendar in Monday.com, briefs and first drafts that I then edited, A/B tests, localized content, and do's and don'ts docs the whole team could follow.

I set up the calendar to do some of the work itself. Each item had a Generate button. Clicking it ran a Zapier workflow that sent the item to Claude, with instructions built from our do's and don'ts, and wrote the result back into the board for me to edit.

93 Seconds of Takes, Cut to 43

I was given raw footage for a Mars Men project: six takes, with two hooks, three body takes and a call to action. I started by giving ChatGPT the transcript of every take. It told me what to cut and helped me pick the hook. Then I cut the video in Premiere by deleting those lines from the transcript, used ChatGPT to find B-roll on Pexels that fit what I had in mind, and let AI make the captions for sound-off viewing.

One Episode, Five Pieces

A demonstration on a public episode of Cohesity's Resilience Universe podcast: CEO Sanjay Poonen with Kevin Mandia, 26 minutes. Claude reads the transcript and marks the moments that make sense on their own. I cut each one to the shape and length of the place it will run.

Written for Search Engines and AI Answers

People type keywords into Google and ask ChatGPT full questions. GEO and AEO, generative and answer engine optimization, mean writing pages that work for both.

At QAD Redzone in 2024 I organized our FAQs and blogs so search engines and AI answers could find them. At Nike I wrote alt text and product tags for both kinds of search: for someone who knows exactly what they want, and for someone who only knows roughly.
