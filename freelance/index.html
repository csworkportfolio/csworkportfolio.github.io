Work Outside the Day Job

Working inside a structured brand often means playing it safe. Outside full-time roles I took paid commissions with real deadlines, and built concepts of my own to try tones, layouts and identities that wouldn't fit a corporate brief.

Paid Client Work

Two paid commissions: an Instagram Story promo for LANY's show at Stubb's in Austin, and an animated graphic for a runner's Boston Marathon fundraiser with Team JAF (Joe Andruzzi Foundation), which raised over $13,000 for families facing cancer. Alongside them is a YouTube thumbnail for a creator's series on mid-century design.

Foundations in Strategy and Storytelling at Texas A&M

At Texas A&M's Mays Business School, I was introduced to where business strategy meets human behaviour, and I got hooked on how design could shape both. These projects were my first attempts at making ideas visual and actionable. Each took on a different challenge, from reviving struggling brands to finding behavioural insights in food safety, social platforms and product design.

The tools were simple and the constraints were tight, but the focus was the same as now: clarity, with the user first. These projects are where my creative instincts started.
